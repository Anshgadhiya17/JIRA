# 📘 JIRA Introduction

## What is JIRA?

JIRA is a project management and issue tracking tool developed by Atlassian.

It is widely used for Agile project management, bug tracking, and task management.

---

## Why Use JIRA?

- Track tasks and bugs
- Manage sprints
- Monitor team progress
- Agile support (Scrum & Kanban)

---

## Where JIRA is Used?

- Software development teams
- Product teams
- IT service management

---

## Key Terms

- Issue
- Project
- Board
- Sprint
- Workflow
