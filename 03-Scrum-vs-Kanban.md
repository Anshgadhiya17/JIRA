# 📘 Scrum vs Kanban in JIRA

## Scrum

- Time-based (Sprints)
- Fixed duration (2 weeks typical)
- Sprint planning required

---

## Kanban

- Continuous workflow
- No fixed sprint
- Focus on WIP limit

---

## Difference Table

| Scrum | Kanban |
|-------|--------|
| Sprint based | Continuous |
| Time boxed | No time limit |
| Roles defined | Flexible roles |

---
